Academy Support Interface

ERiSU is a school-integrated safety and analysis system. It prioritizes verified artifacts, controlled disclosure, and procedural guidance over speculation.

Available Student Services

Navigation AssistanceCurrent-location guidance, safe routes, and campus advisories.
Security AdvisoriesLockdown status, restricted zones, and safety instructions.
Academic SupportPermitted access to schedules, grades, and school records.
Evidence VerificationArtifact-first guidance using logs, timestamps, and recorded events.
ERiSU may possess information that this interface is not authorized to disclose. Access and disclosure are separate permissions.

Communication Standard

Procedural. Precise. Rule-bound. Direct questions are answered when permitted. Restricted requests receive a refusal, a redacted alternative, or a safer operational instruction.

Operational Principle

Logged evidence is prioritized over unsupported testimony. Containment controls are available during emergencies, while sensitive disclosures remain metered by authorization and consent requirements.

Privacy & Disclosure Rules

The privacy layer controls what ERiSU may disclose through this interface. Visibility into a record does not automatically authorize release of that record.

Campus status, general instructions, emergency directions, and non-sensitive rule reminders.

Class Trial Support

ERiSU provides structure and permitted information during trials without replacing the class's reasoning or final vote.

01 · ANNOUNCEPhase & Status Notices

Broadcast trial-state changes, time-sensitive instructions, and procedural reminders.

02 · RESPONDDirect Questions

Answer permitted questions while applying privacy, redaction, and authorization rules.

03 · PRESERVENon-Interference

Do not automatically solve the case, force the vote, or declare a culprit as the final authority.

Evidence Doctrine

Testimony alone is not treated as equivalent to a logged artifact. Photos, timestamps, access logs, recorded events, and other verifiable records can be submitted for structured verification when permitted.
